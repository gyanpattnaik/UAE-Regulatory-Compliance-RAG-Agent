## Part 1 — Risk Governance

Article 1 — Board Responsibilities
1.1 The Board of Directors of every VASP shall be ultimately responsible for the sound and prudent management of the VASP, including oversight of all virtual asset activities and associated risks.

1.2 The Board shall approve and periodically review (at least annually):
- The VASP's risk appetite statement
- The compliance and risk management frameworks
- The AML/CFT policies and procedures
- Business continuity and disaster recovery plans

1.3 At least two members of the Board shall demonstrate relevant experience in virtual asset technology, financial services regulation, or risk management.

Article 2 — Risk Management Framework
2.1 Every VASP shall establish and maintain a comprehensive risk management framework commensurate with the nature, scale, and complexity of its virtual asset activities.

2.2 The risk management framework shall address, at a minimum:
- Market risk arising from virtual asset price volatility
- Operational risk including technology failures and human error
- Liquidity risk including the ability to meet customer withdrawal requests
- Counterparty risk in relation to exchange and settlement activities
- Legal and regulatory risk including cross-jurisdictional compliance
- Reputational risk

2.3 VASPs shall maintain a dedicated risk management function, independent of business operations, with direct reporting access to the Board.

## Part 2 — Compliance Requirements

Article 3 — Compliance Officer
3.1 Every VASP shall appoint a qualified Compliance Officer who shall be:
- A UAE resident
- A person of good standing with no criminal convictions related to financial crimes
- Experienced in financial services compliance, AML/CFT, or virtual asset regulation
- Independent from revenue-generating functions

3.2 The Compliance Officer shall be responsible for:
- Monitoring compliance with all applicable VARA regulations
- Submitting regulatory reports and filings to VARA
- Managing the VASP's AML/CFT program
- Conducting internal compliance reviews at least quarterly
- Reporting compliance breaches to the Board and VARA within 48 hours

Article 4 — Regulatory Reporting
4.1 VASPs shall submit the following reports to VARA:
- Monthly transaction volume reports (within 15 business days of month-end)
- Quarterly financial statements (within 30 business days of quarter-end)
- Annual audited financial statements (within 90 business days of year-end)
- Immediate notification of material events (within 24 hours)

4.2 Material events that require immediate notification include:
- Any security breach affecting customer assets or data
- Insolvency or material deterioration in financial condition
- Any regulatory action by another authority (domestic or foreign)
- Changes in beneficial ownership exceeding 10% of voting rights
- Suspension or termination of any significant business activity

Article 5 — Market Conduct
5.1 VASPs shall not engage in or facilitate:
- Market manipulation, including wash trading, spoofing, or layering
- Insider trading based on material non-public information
- Front-running of customer orders
- Artificial inflation or deflation of virtual asset prices

5.2 VASPs operating exchange services shall implement surveillance systems capable of detecting and reporting suspicious trading patterns.

5.3 Violations of market conduct rules shall result in penalties including fines of up to AED 20,000,000, license suspension, or referral to criminal authorities.

## Part 3 — Audit and Examination

Article 6 — Internal Audit
6.1 VASPs with total customer assets exceeding AED 50,000,000 shall maintain an internal audit function independent of management.

6.2 The internal audit function shall conduct risk-based audits of:
- AML/CFT compliance
- Customer asset segregation and custody controls
- Technology and cybersecurity controls
- Financial reporting integrity
- Complaints handling effectiveness

Article 7 — External Audit
7.1 All VASPs shall engage an external auditor approved by VARA to conduct annual audits.

7.2 The external auditor shall provide an opinion on:
- The VASP's financial statements
- The adequacy of internal controls over customer assets
- Compliance with VARA's capital adequacy requirements
- The integrity of the VASP's virtual asset reserves relative to customer balances (Proof of Reserves)

Article 8 — VARA Examination Powers
8.1 VARA may at any time conduct on-site or off-site examinations of any VASP.
8.2 VASPs shall provide VARA with unrestricted access to all records, systems, and personnel during examinations.
8.3 Failure to cooperate with a VARA examination shall result in immediate license suspension.
