CENTRAL BANK OF THE UAE
FEDERAL DECREE-LAW NO. 6 OF 2025
ON THE CENTRAL BANK AND THE ORGANIZATION OF FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS AND ACTIVITIES

Chapter I — Preliminary Provisions

Article 1 — Title

This Decree-Law shall be cited as the "Central Bank and Financial Institutions and Activities Law."

Article 2 — Definitions

In applying the provisions of this Decree-Law, the following words and expressions shall have the meanings assigned to them hereunder, unless the context requires otherwise:

1. "State" means the United Arab Emirates.
2. "Central Bank" means the Central Bank of the UAE established under this Decree-Law.
3. "Board" means the Board of Directors of the Central Bank.
4. "Governor" means the Governor of the Central Bank.
5. "Licensed Financial Institution" means any person licensed by the Central Bank to carry out any Licensed Financial Activity.
6. "Licensed Financial Activity" means any financial activity for which a license is required under the provisions of this Decree-Law.
7. "AML" means Anti-Money Laundering.
8. "CFT" means Countering the Financing of Terrorism.

Chapter II — Establishment and Objectives of the Central Bank

Article 3 — Establishment

A public institution with legal personality shall be established, having financial and administrative independence, called the "Central Bank of the United Arab Emirates."

Article 4 — Objectives

The Central Bank shall seek to achieve the following main objectives:
a. Formulate and implement monetary policy in the State to maintain price stability.
b. Regulate, develop, and oversee financial institutions and their activities.
c. Maintain the stability of the financial system.
d. Develop and promote an efficient, competitive, and stable financial services sector.

Chapter III — Obligations of Licensed Financial Institutions

Article 15 — General Obligations

Every Licensed Financial Institution shall:

1. Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, standards, and instructions issued by the Central Bank.
2. Maintain adequate capital and liquidity buffers as determined by the Central Bank.
3. Implement robust AML/CFT frameworks in accordance with applicable laws.
4. Submit periodic reports to the Central Bank in the manner and form prescribed by it.
5. Maintain proper books of accounts and records.

Article 16 — Reporting Requirements

1. Licensed Financial Institutions shall report all suspicious transactions to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) within 24 hours of detection.
2. Licensed Financial Institutions must submit quarterly financial reports to the Central Bank no later than 30 days after the end of each quarter.
3. Annual audited financial statements must be submitted within 90 days of the financial year end.
4. Any material breach or significant operational incident must be reported to the Central Bank within 24 hours of the institution becoming aware.

Article 17 — Record Keeping

Licensed Financial Institutions are required to:
a. Maintain complete records of all transactions for a minimum period of five (5) years.
b. Ensure records are readily retrievable upon request by the Central Bank.
c. Store records in a secure manner that protects their integrity and confidentiality.

Chapter IV — Licensing and Authorization

Article 20 — Licensing Requirements

1. No person may conduct any Licensed Financial Activity in the State without obtaining the appropriate license from the Central Bank.
2. License applications shall be submitted in the form prescribed by the Central Bank, together with all required documents and fees.
3. The Central Bank may approve, reject, or impose conditions on any license application.
4. Licenses are not transferable unless approved by the Central Bank.

Article 21 — Fit and Proper Requirements

All owners, directors, senior management, and key function holders of Licensed Financial Institutions shall meet the fit and proper requirements established by the Central Bank, including:
- Good character and integrity
- Adequate professional qualifications and experience
- No criminal convictions related to financial crimes or dishonesty
- No unresolved conflicts of interest

Article 22 — Penalties

Violation of the provisions of this Decree-Law may result in:
1. Administrative penalties up to AED 50,000,000.
2. Suspension or revocation of license.
3. Prohibition from holding management positions.
4. Public disclosure of violations.
